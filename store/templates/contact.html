Where is my order?

Once your order is placed you'll see its status change from Processing to Shipped. Include your order number in the form on this page and we can look it up for you.

How can I track my order?

Use the "Track My Order" option below with your order number, or reach out to support and we'll send you an update.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa and Cash on Delivery, with card payments coming soon.

Can I return a product?

Yes — reach out with your order number and reason for return and our team will guide you through the process.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by location, but most orders within Kenya arrive within a few business days of processing.

How do I change my delivery address?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact us as soon as possible with the correct address and your order number.
